Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

ML Grind is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Service.

The short version: your Python code runs in your browser, and by default your progress and code stay there. If you sign in with Google, your progress and saved code are synced to our database. If you ask for AI feedback, that submission is sent to be reviewed. Usage analytics (Google Analytics) can be turned off below. We do not sell your data.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally (Your Browser)

By default, the following data is saved in your browser's localStorage:

  • Progress data: which problems you've started or solved, when, and in which track (NumPy or PyTorch)
  • Saved code: your code for each problem, in each track
  • Scratchpad code: code you've written in the Python scratchpad
  • Flash card progress: which cards you've reviewed and when each is next due (synced to your account too when you are signed in)
  • Preferences: light or dark theme, your preferred track, which sidebar groups are open, and whether analytics is turned off
  • Analytics markers: your last visit date and whether you've run or passed a first exercise, used to count returning visits and first runs

Progress and saved code are synced to Google Cloud Firestore only if you sign in (see below). Requesting AI feedback sends the current submission for review. Otherwise, everything listed here stays in your browser.

Feedback Submissions

When you submit feedback through our feedback form, we collect:

  • The name you enter
  • Your email address (optional, only if you provide it)
  • The feedback message you write

This information is sent via Formspree and is used solely to respond to your feedback and improve the Service.

Google Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google (through Firebase Authentication), we store the following in Google Cloud Firestore to sync your progress across devices:

  • Google profile information: your name, email address, and profile photo URL
  • Progress data: problem status, saved code for each track, flash card review schedules, and timestamps, plus the time of your last sync
  • Sign-in log: each sign-in records your account ID, name, email, profile photo URL, the time, and your browser's user-agent string, for security and to understand usage

Database rules let your signed-in account read and write only your own progress. The site operator can also access this data to run and support the Service. You can use every feature of ML Grind without signing in; your data then stays in your browser.

Analytics and Hosting

Optional usage analytics help us understand which exercises work well. We respect your browser’s Do Not Track preference.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the Service is used. It records page views and usage events: opening exercises and learning paths, running code, passing tests, first runs and passes, sharing a challenge, signing in, and returning on another day. Events carry exercise, section, and path identifiers and the track you used, never your code, test inputs, or results. Google Analytics also collects standard technical data such as approximate location, browser and device type, and the referring page, and may set cookies. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. When you are signed in, events are associated with a pseudonymous account ID (not your name or email).

Analytics does not load at all if you turn it off above or your browser sends Do Not Track.

ML Grind is hosted on GitHub Pages, loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and downloads the Python runtime (Pyodide) from the jsDelivr CDN. These providers receive standard request data such as your IP address and browser. GitHub may keep server logs including IP addresses and page requests; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not require an account; signing in is optional
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers
  • We do not sell your data, and we share it only with the service providers named in this policy, to run the Service

Lecture Videos

Section pages offer an optional animated lecture hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play; the player then uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) mode, and Google's privacy policy applies to the video.

AI Feedback (Optional)

When you open the AI Feedback tab after running tests, your current code, the exercise title and description, and each test's description, expected value, actual value and pass/fail result are sent to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards them to Anthropic's API to generate the review. We do not store these submissions; Cloudflare and Anthropic process them under their own policies. Nothing is sent unless you open that tab.

Code Execution

All Python code execution happens entirely in your browser using Pyodide (WebAssembly). Your code is never sent to any external server for execution. This means:

  • Running Python and tests does not send your code to a server
  • Execution output and test results stay in your browser
  • Only optional cloud sync and AI feedback transmit the data described above

Data Retention

Local data: Data stored in your browser persists until you clear your browser's localStorage or use a browser cleaning tool.

Synced data: Progress synced to Firestore and the sign-in log are kept until you ask us to delete them.

Feedback: Feedback submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and improve the Service.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your local data at any time through your browser settings
  • Use the Service without providing any personal information
  • Turn off usage analytics (above)
  • Ask for a copy of, or deletion of, your synced account data and sign-in records by emailing us
  • Request deletion of any feedback you've submitted

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for general audiences interested in learning machine learning. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. No account is required, so the Service can be used without providing any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
