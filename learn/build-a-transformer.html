Build a transformer

Build every part of a transformer in NumPy, then put the parts together. You will write attention, masking, multi-head projections, positional information and normalisation, assemble an encoder block, train a character-level GPT, and debug a broken one.

10 problems · about 4.5 h · Intermediate. You'll need: NumPy and a basic neural network (forward pass and softmax).

By the end you can

  • Write scaled dot-product and multi-head attention with correct shapes and masking
  • Explain why transformers need positional information and compare sinusoidal encodings with RoPE
  • Assemble, train and debug a small GPT from your own components

Steps

  1. Step 1: Scaled Dot-Product Attention → The core operation: queries, keys, values and the 1/√d scale.
  2. Step 2: Causal Attention Mask → Stop each position from attending to the future.
  3. Step 3: Multi-Head Attention → Split into heads, attend in parallel and project back.
  4. Step 4: Sinusoidal Positional Encoding → Sinusoidal encodings: give attention a sense of order.
  5. Step 5: Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) → Rotary embeddings, the positional scheme most current LLMs use.
  6. Step 6: Layer Normalization → LayerNorm and where it sits in a pre-norm block.
  7. Step 7: BPE Tokenization → Byte-pair encoding: how text becomes token ids.
  8. Step 8: E2E: Transformer Encoder → Assemble a full encoder block: attention, MLP, residuals and norms.
  9. Step 9: Mini GPT (Character-Level Language Model) → Train a character-level GPT and generate text from it.
  10. Step 10: Transformer Bug Hunt: Fix a Broken Mini-GPT → Find and fix the bugs in a broken mini GPT.
