## Logos

Logos come from the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) npm package (CC0 for the package; each mark remains a trademark of its owner, see its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md)). They are drawn small, monochrome in the current text colour, never recoloured, distorted or animated, and always next to the company name. The site shows: "Based on public interview reports; logos are trademarks of their owners and don't imply endorsement." Simple Icons no longer ships Amazon, AWS, OpenAI or Microsoft marks, so those companies get a neutral monogram, as do companies Simple Icons does not cover. Uber also gets the monogram because its Simple Icons mark is a wordmark that is unreadable at 12-14px. Brand guidelines worth a glance: Pinterest, Datadog, Meta and Databricks link theirs from the Simple Icons data file.
